  • In this role, you will apply measurement science to real-world industrial and laboratory situations in a GMP manufacturing environment, designing, developing, and evaluating measurement systems and procedures to facilitate the calibration of a wide variety of instruments. The Opportunity
  • You have a proven knowledge of computerized calibration management systems (CCMS), spreadsheets, database applications, and quality management systems within an FDA-regulated industry.
  • You will utilize advanced technical writing skills and meticulous attention to detail to manage multiple calibration tasks and meet critical deadlines.
  • You will directly influence operations by dictating calibration and metrological policy through scientific, mathematical, or regulatory means.
